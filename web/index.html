ℹ️ About the model
  • Architecture: ResNet18 pre-trained on ImageNet, fine-tuned with transfer learning
  • Data: PlantVillage (54,305 images, 38 classes)
  • Test performance: 98.5% accuracy, 0.981 macro F1-score
  • Runtime: the model (ONNX format) runs directly in your browser
  • Limitation: trained on lab photos (single leaf, plain background). Field photos are recognized less reliably.
